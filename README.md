## 1. Project Overview

ManifestoESG is a custom WordPress theme developed for an organization dedicated to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles. The platform serves as an institutional hub for publishing reports, articles, and corporate guidelines, built with a focus on accessibility and structured content management via Advanced Custom Fields Pro.

## 2. The Problem

Organizations focused on ESG need their digital presence to reflect their core values: transparency, accessibility, and sustainability. A bloated, slow-loading template with poor accessibility would contradict the organization's mission. Furthermore, they needed a robust way to publish and categorize complex reports and articles that standard WordPress posts couldn't gracefully handle without heavy modification.

---

## 3. The Solution & Architecture

A lean, purpose-built WordPress theme that prioritizes semantic HTML, accessibility, and structured data management.

### Architecture

- **Custom Post Types (CPTs):** Dedicated CPTs for specific content types such as 'Reports' and 'Initiatives', allowing for distinct archive and single-view presentations.
- **ACF Field Groups:** Programmatically defined field groups for complex content structures, ensuring editors input data consistently.
- **Performance-Oriented:** Avoidance of heavy page builders in favor of custom PHP templates, reducing the carbon footprint of the site (aligning with the 'E' in ESG) through faster load times and less data transfer.

---

## 4. Technologies Used

- **CMS & Backend:** WordPress 6.0+, PHP 8.0+, MySQL
- **Content Management:** ACF Pro, Custom Post Types
- **Styling:** Custom CSS focusing on WCAG accessibility guidelines

---

## 5. Design Process & UI/UX

The design language is clean, modern, and highly legible, with a strong emphasis on accessibility (color contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility). The user experience is tailored around content discovery, making it easy for stakeholders, journalists, and the public to find specific ESG reports and guidelines.

## 6. Project Outcomes

- **Value Alignment:** A digital platform whose performance and accessibility reflect the organization's ESG commitments.
- **Structured Publishing:** A robust backend architecture that simplifies the publishing of complex corporate reports.
- **Editorial Independence:** The communications team can manage all aspects of the site without developer intervention.
